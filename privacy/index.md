# FiloFic Privacy Policy

FiloFic collects no data. Everything you add is kept on your device, encrypted, and FiloFic never sends it anywhere.

## Nothing is collected

FiloFic has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer never sees what you keep in it. If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage figures; FiloFic adds nothing to them.

## Kept on your device

The fics you import, with the original file of each EPUB, their covers, and your tags, ratings, notes and reading progress are kept in one library on this device, encrypted with AES-256. A few display preferences, such as the theme and the library layout, are kept in FiloFic's settings on the device, which iOS includes in its backups.

## Backups and your recovery passphrase

Without a recovery passphrase, your library isn't included in device backups and isn't stored in iCloud. If you set one in Settings › Backup, the library, still encrypted, is included in this device's backups, and in iCloud Backup if it's turned on, so it can be restored to a new device. Its key is kept beside it, locked with your passphrase, which FiloFic doesn't store. Nobody can open the library without the passphrase: not the developer, not Apple, not anyone who has the backup. You can also choose to save the passphrase in your iCloud Keychain, end-to-end encrypted; it's off unless you turn it on. With it on, someone with your Apple Account and a device passcode could open your library from a backup. Export Library makes one file of the encrypted library, locked the same way, which goes only where you send it.

## No internet connection

FiloFic never connects to the internet. It doesn't download stories, check for anything or send anything. Backups and iCloud Keychain are iOS's own, and iOS sends them, not FiloFic. An EPUB can ask for pictures, fonts or pages from the internet; FiloFic blocks every such request, so a book shows only what's inside it. The Support and Release Notes links in Settings, the link to Apple's page in Authors' Rights and AI, a fic's link to its page on the archive it came from, and the web links in a fic's text open in your browser, not in FiloFic.

## Face ID or Touch ID

The key to your library is kept in this device's Keychain and released only when Face ID or Touch ID recognises you. iOS does the check and tells FiloFic only whether it matched: FiloFic never sees your face or fingerprint.

## Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is off until you turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence. With it on, on iPhone and iPad models that support it, FiloFic can suggest a fic's fandom, ship, mood and a short summary, and understand searches written in your own words. This uses Apple's model on the device itself: nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else.

## Sharing is up to you

A fic leaves FiloFic only when you share it or send it to your Kindle, and the library only when you export it. It then goes through the app you choose, such as Mail or Files, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Deleting everything

Delete a fic from its page to remove it from the library. To delete everything, use Clear Library in Settings, which also removes your recovery passphrase and any copy of it in iCloud Keychain, or delete the FiloFic app. Device backups made while a passphrase was set, and library files you exported, keep their encrypted copy until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on FiloFic's privacy page, with the date it changed.
