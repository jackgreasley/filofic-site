# FiloFic Privacy Policy

FiloFic collects no data. Everything you add stays on your device, encrypted, and is never sent anywhere.

## Nothing is collected

FiloFic has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer never sees what you keep in it. If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage figures; FiloFic adds nothing to them.

## Everything stays on your device

The fics you import, their covers, and your tags, ratings, notes and reading progress are kept in one library on this device, encrypted with AES-256. The library isn't stored in iCloud or included in device backups. A few display preferences, such as the theme and the library layout, are kept in FiloFic's settings on the device.

## No internet connection

FiloFic never connects to the internet. It doesn't download stories, check for anything or send anything. The Support link in Settings, a fic's link to its page on the archive it came from, and any web link you tap in a fic's text open in your web browser, not in FiloFic.

## Face ID or Touch ID

The key to your library is kept in this device's Keychain and released only when Face ID or Touch ID recognises you. iOS does the check and tells FiloFic only whether it matched: FiloFic never sees your face or fingerprint.

## Apple Intelligence

On iPhone and iPad models that support Apple Intelligence, with it turned on, FiloFic can suggest a fic's fandom, ship and tone, and understand searches written in your own words. This uses Apple's model on the device itself: nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else.

## Sharing is up to you

A fic leaves FiloFic only when you share it or send it to your Kindle. It then goes through the app you choose, such as Mail, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Deleting everything

Delete a fic from its page to remove it from the library. To delete everything, delete the FiloFic app: press and hold its icon, then choose Remove App and Delete App. The library and everything in it are gone for good, as nothing is kept anywhere else.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on FiloFic's privacy page, with the date it changed.
