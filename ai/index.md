# Authors' Rights and AI

Also in: [Español](es-419/) · [Português](pt-BR/) · [Bahasa Indonesia](id/) · [日本語](ja/) · [한국어](ko/)

Last updated 8 November 2026.

**Your fics stay yours, and they're never used to train AI. Not by us, not by anyone we
work with, ever.**

Fan fiction is written by people, for people, and given away for love. FiloFic is built to
keep it that way.

## What we promise

- **We never train AI on fics.** We have no AI model of our own, and we've never trained
  one on fan fiction or anything else.
- **We never collect fics.** FiloFic has no servers and no account. The fics you import stay
  on your device, encrypted. We can't see them, and nobody else can either.
- **We never let fics be collected.** FiloFic never connects to the internet. It has no
  analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends your library to no one. EPUBs are
  shown in a reader that's cut off from the network, so a book can't fetch or send anything.
- **A fic leaves FiloFic only when you choose.** Sharing a fic, sending it to your Kindle,
  or exporting your library are all things you do yourself, and they go only where you send
  them.

## Apple Intelligence: off unless you turn it on

FiloFic can use Apple Intelligence to suggest a fic's fandom, ship, mood and a short
summary, and to understand searches written in your own words. It's **off until you turn
it on**, in Settings › Apple Intelligence. While it's off, no AI reads any of your fics,
summaries, tags or searches.

If you turn it on:

- it uses Apple's model, which runs on your device. Your fics aren't sent to Apple or to
  anyone else;
- it reads a fic's title, tags, summary and opening pages to make its suggestions, and
  nothing more;
- Apple says it doesn't use people's private data or interactions to train its models
  ([Apple Machine Learning Research](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/introducing-apple-foundation-models));
- you can turn it off at any time, and remove everything it suggested with Settings ›
  Apple Intelligence › Remove Suggested Details.

FiloFic doesn't make or control Apple's model. What it was trained on is Apple's to answer;
Apple describes it in the page above.

## Backups

If you set a recovery passphrase, your library is included in your device's backups, still
encrypted. Nobody can open it without your passphrase: not us, not Apple, and not any AI.

## How FiloFic was made

We'd rather you heard it from us. FiloFic was built with the help of AI coding tools, as
most apps are now. While building it, we tested importing, search and categorising on a
collection of publicly posted fan fiction files kept on our own computer, and the AI coding
tools read some of them while we tested. We didn't train any AI model on them, they aren't
in the app, and they never appear in our screenshots, which show stories we invented.

## Questions

If you have a question about this, or think we've got something wrong, contact us through
the support page. We'll answer, and if we change anything here, we'll say so on this page
and in the release notes.
