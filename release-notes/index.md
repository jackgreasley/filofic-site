# What's new in FiloFic

The newest release comes first.

## Beta 202611121834 · 12 November 2026

- **Search keeps the keyboard.** In a fic whose title takes two lines, the keyboard came up
  for the search and went straight away again. It stays now.
- **Faster search in long fics.** Results come a thousand at a time, in reading order, as
  soon as they're found. Scroll to the end of the list for the next thousand. In
  Shakespeare's Complete Works, a common word's first thousand come in a moment, where
  listing them all took many seconds.

## Beta 202611120835 · 12 November 2026

- **Search within a fic.** With the bars showing, swipe down on the top bar to search the
  fic you're reading. Matches are listed by chapter, each among the words around it; tap
  one to go there, and step through the rest with the arrows.
- **Furthest, rebuilt.** The furthest point is wherever you've got to, however you got
  there: reading, Hands Free, the scrubber or a jump. It's kept the moment you're there,
  even if FiloFic is closed straight after. Go back a few paragraphs, show the bars, and
  Furthest takes you back. In a long fic too.
- **A calm start.** Opening FiloFic shows only the logo. "Unlocking your library" appears
  only if unlocking takes more than a second, and the Unlock button only if Face ID is
  cancelled.
- **Taps between chapters.** In an EPUB, a tap in the space between two chapters, or above
  the end of the fic, shows the bars.

## Beta 202611120432 · 12 November 2026

- **Highlights and bookmarks.** Hold some text and Highlight it in one tap, in the colour
  you chose last; the colour button beside it opens the swatches. Tap a highlight to change
  its colour or take it off. Bookmark from the same menu, and a small blue bookmark marks its
  first word.
  - The bookmark at the top shows your bookmarks in the text or hides them. Hold it for the
    list of bookmarks and highlights, with Previous and Next to go from one to the next.
  - The fic's page lists them, the latest first, each among the words around it. Swipe one
    to take it off.
- **Selections in EPUBs stretch.** Drag a selection's handles to take in more words.
- **Back to where you'd got to.** Read back a way, tap for the bars, and the progress pill
  offers Furthest, which takes you back. Read Again and Mark as Unread start afresh.
- **The fic's page** opens on its cover, title and Continue, as large as fits, and shrinks
  them to a bar as you scroll down to everything else.
- **Works in progress keep up.** Update from File, in a fic's ⋯ menu, brings it up to date
  from a newer download, with your place, highlights, bookmarks, tags and reading history
  kept. An older file, or one that seems another fic, is checked with you first, side by side
  with yours. Highlights and bookmarks whose words aren't in the new version are kept and
  marked, and you're told.
  - Importing a folder does the same: several downloads of one fic come in once, as the
    latest, and fics already in your library are brought up to date, never taken back. The
    summary says which, by title.
- **At the end of a fic from AO3,** after its last words: a word about your read, then
  Kudos, Comment, the work on AO3 and more by its author. Kudos and Comment are in the
  reader's progress pill too.
- **Where you left off** has a button to the fic's page.

## Beta 202611110312 · 11 November 2026

- **Find Series shows covers.** Each suggested part has its cover beside it, and tapping one
  shows its details, then swipe to see the series' other fics in order. The same on a
  series' Edit list.
- **An import's summary goes by itself** after a few seconds.
- **Hands Free starts faster,** at 50 lines a minute instead of 20. A speed you've chosen
  stays as it is.
- **Much less room on your device.** A fic kept as its EPUB no longer keeps a second copy of
  its text: the EPUB is its text, and search finds its words in an index that keeps none of
  its own. On a big library that's close to half of FiloFic's size.
  - It's set up in the background after you update, a batch at a time, with search working
    throughout. The next time you unlock after that, FiloFic gives the room back to your
    device, which takes a moment, once: it says "Making room on your device".
  - If a fic's EPUB can't be shown, the reader says so.
- **Fics' details read as they should, from every archive.** Titles lose the site's words
  around them, "| FanFiction", "[Archive of Our Own]", "| SpaceBattles", "- Wattpad", along
  with chapter numbers and tags like "(Complete)", "[WIP]" or "(Worm/OC)"; the author comes
  from the title when the page names one there. Garbled characters such as "Donâ€™t" are put
  right, and stray HTML, BBCode, Markdown and invisible characters go, from titles, authors,
  fandoms, ships, tags, summaries and series. Fics already in your library are tidied in the
  background.

## Beta 202611102013 · 10 November 2026

- **Big libraries are fast.** Made for libraries of 500 fics and more.
  - Typing in search is always smooth: the results follow a moment after you stop typing,
    and never hold up the keyboard.
  - The library loads in a tenth of the time, and searching inside the text of every fic is
    many times quicker. The first unlock after updating takes a moment longer, once, while
    FiloFic sets this up.
  - Library, Calendar and You switch instantly, and each comes back just as you left it.
- **The fic you're reading opens at once.** FiloFic gets it ready while Where you left off
  shows, so Resume goes straight to the text. Every fic opens sooner too.
- **Where you left off stays with you.** Scroll down the library and the card shrinks to a
  line with the fic's name and Resume, which stays at the top; scroll back up and it grows
  into the card again.
- **The fic you're reading never disappears.** With Hide Explicit Fics on, a fic you're
  partway through is no longer hidden because categorising guessed it spicy. The archive's
  Explicit rating, or a Spiciness score you set yourself, still hides it.
- **Tidier details from more archives.**
  - Every HTML character name is read, so titles like "TtH &bull; Story &bull; The Wish"
    become "The Wish".
  - Fandoms, ships and tags written as links, as SquidgeWorld's downloads have them, show as
    plain names.
  - A title that's an archive's whole page title, "Title - author - fandom", is split into
    the title and the author.
  - Fics already in your library are tidied in the background.
- **Hands Free's guide waits for the text.** It fades in once the words are on the page,
  never over an empty screen.
- **Settings shows FiloFic's real size,** everything it keeps on the device, as iPhone
  Storage counts it.
- **Less room on your device.**
  - Covers take about a third of the room they did. Existing covers are made smaller in the
    background.
  - Nothing is left behind in temporary storage: an exported library file goes when the
    library locks, and anything else left over goes when FiloFic next opens.
- **Test builds:** Developer has a Storage section showing where FiloFic's room goes, set
  against what iPhone Storage counts, with every table and index in the library measured on
  request.

## Beta 202611100539 · 10 November 2026

- **Hands Free reading.** A third paging choice beside Scroll and Paginated, in the reader's Aa
  sheet and on You.
  - Tap the big Play button and the text moves up by itself at the lines a minute you choose,
    from 1 to 200, 20 to start with. Hold + or − on You to change it quickly.
  - A tap on the page stops it and brings back the bars. Choosing a chapter, opening Aa or
    leaving the app stops it too, and dragging the text takes over by hand.
  - It runs on through the chapters, stops at the end of the fic, and keeps the screen on while
    it moves.
  - A band across the middle of the screen lights up the line passing through it, fading over
    the lines either side, to help you keep your place. It looks alike on every page colour,
    in blue, green, yellow, rose or violet, or it can be off, on You and in the Aa sheet.
  - You's Preview scrolls a longer passage at the chosen speed.
- **Colour on You:** the reader's Light, Paper, Grey and Dark pages, beside Paging.
- **Paging** is the Aa sheet's name for Scroll, Paginated and Hands Free, as on You.
- **A new look, and a tab bar.** Library, Calendar and You sit along the bottom.
  - **You** has your reading at a glance, the reader's text size, font and paging with a
    preview, the theme, the library's layout and covers, and your tags, with New Tag first.
  - **Settings** is behind the gear on You, in order: Backup, Apple Intelligence, your data,
    Kindle, a Danger Zone holding both clears, then About. Its explanations are behind ?
    buttons instead of under the rows.
  - **A blue dot on the gear** says the library isn't backed up yet, and the same dot in
    Settings marks where.
- **A simpler library header:** + for Import, Add Fic and Find Series, and Select Fics beside
  it. Sort is in the filter sheet and the layouts are on You.
- **Where you left off** heads the library as a card. Tap it to read on, or long-press it for
  the fic's page.
- **Continue names the real chapter,** a preface included, as the fic's chapter list does.
- **Tags can be deleted** from the tag's editor.
- **Find Series** is the new name for Auto Series, in the menu and on its sheet.
- **Restore from a Library File** has moved into Settings, under Backup.
- **The Calendar's details** just open the fic: Go to That Day has gone.

## Beta 202611092217 · 9 November 2026

- **Big books import in a moment.** Shakespeare's Complete Works, 900 chapters, used to take
  most of a minute to read and now takes about a second. Every EPUB, HTML and web page imports
  faster too.
- **Add Fic Manually says when it's reading a file,** and Save waits until it's done.
- **What an import did shows as a toast** at the top of the library, which goes by itself,
  instead of an alert you have to close.
- **Every fic gets a summary when it's categorised, adult fics too.** A fic's own summary
  always comes first, including the one AO3 prints on a fic's first pages. When Apple
  Intelligence won't write one, the fic's opening lines stand in. Only when there's nothing
  at all does it say so.
- **Categorising is quicker.**
  - A fic whose tags already name its moods, such as Angst, Fluff or Hurt/Comfort, gets them
    from its tags without Apple Intelligence.
  - Fics that come with a summary are read in less time.
  - On a test library, a third of the fics needed no Apple Intelligence at all.
- **Tiny is the summary text size** to start with. Settings › Library › Summary text changes
  it.
- **Assign Tag groups your tags:** the ones you made first, then FiloFic's, then the ones that
  came with the fic, each under its own heading.
- **Clear Library shows how it's getting on** over the whole screen until your new library
  opens, instead of leaving you looking at the old one.
- **The book's own cover is always a choice.** After using a picture from inside an EPUB as
  the cover, Choose a Cover still offers the original, so you can have it back.
- **Pictures open fitted to the screen,** below their buttons, which stay easy to see even
  over a white page, and without a jump.
- **A quieter empty library,** with just its buttons.
- **The Calendar sums your reading** instead of listing every session. A day says "3 fics ·
  10 sessions · 2h 2m · 5K words" and shows each fic once, and a fic's details sum all its
  reads, so each fits on one screen.

## Beta 202611090831 · 9 November 2026

- **Mark as read from the library.** Swipe a fic's card to mark it read or unread, or use its
  long-press menu. Undo is right there. It doesn't add anything to your Calendar.
- **Smaller summaries, more of them.** Settings › Library › Summary text: Tiny, Small, Normal
  or Large.
- **Favourite authors.** Heart an author on their page, and a Favourite Authors tab shows
  their fics.
- **Heart a series** on its page, or several at once in Select. Loved series show in the
  Loved tab.
- **Series, clearer.**
  - Auto Series says "2 of 13 parts", labels each fic as a part, and shows where parts are
    missing.
  - Look Again searches your library for series once more.
  - Tap any fic's title while making or editing a series to see its cover and summary.
  - On a series' page, press and hold a fic, then drag it into place.
- **Assign Tag.** Type to find one of your tags, by its name or its emoji's, and tap to put
  it on the fic. Making a new tag is the last row.
  - Emoji search by name works now ("sad", "fire").
- **Length filters** go from Under 20K to 500K+, in six steps, and you can pick more than one.
- **😫 for Angst.**
- **A grey reader theme**: light grey text on mid grey.
- **Pictures in EPUBs.** Tap beside a picture to close it, pinch or double-tap to zoom, save
  it to Photos or Files, or use it as the fic's cover.
- **On iPad,** EPUBs no longer slide under the sidebar.
- **Gentler categorising.** It works one fic at a time with rests between, and steps aside in
  Low Power Mode, when your phone is warm and while FiloFic isn't on screen. Pause, Resume
  and Stop are on its status line and in Settings.
- **Search in Japanese, Chinese and Korean** now finds words inside the text, and their word
  counts are right. Your library is updated in the background the first time.
- **Project Gutenberg books get their real chapters,** without the licence pages. EPUBs
  already in your library are split again in the background, keeping your place.
- **Share a book to FiloFic** from Files, including books saved as folders, and Markdown and
  XHTML files.
- **RTF files** can be imported.
- **Thank the author.** Fics from AO3 have Leave Kudos and Comment buttons, which open the
  work in your browser.
- **Fixes:**
  - "&amp;" no longer shows instead of "&" in titles and tags;
  - tags that come with a file are the fic's tags, not your own;
  - Gutenberg subjects read as plain tags ("Whaling ships");
  - the same AO3 work imported twice is caught as a duplicate.

  Fics already in your library are tidied in the background.

## Beta 202611090543 · 9 November 2026

- **The Calendar.** Tap the big Library title to switch to your Calendar, and tap Calendar to
  switch back. It shows what you read and when: a day, a week, a month, a year or everything
  you've ever read, as covers or as book spines on a shelf. A little sun or moon shows whether
  you read by day or at night, and a short line sums up your reading ("Who needs sleep?",
  "Short and sweet.", "A chapter closer."). Tap any book to see each time you read it.
- **Top fics.** The fics you've read most, at the top of All time.
- **Plan what to read next.** The TBR shelf holds every fic you haven't started. Drag them
  into the order you want, and the library's To read tab follows the same order. Up next is
  always the first; Surprise me picks one for you. A series offers its next part only.
- **It starts today.** The Calendar fills in as you read from now on. Fics you read before
  this beta aren't on it.
- **Clear Reading History**, in Settings, deletes your Calendar, reading days and milestones,
  and keeps your fics and where you are in them.
- **For beta testers:** Settings › Reading History can make up a few years of reading, light,
  medium or heavy, from your own library, so you can see the Calendar full. Clear Generated
  History Only takes it away again, keeping your real reading.
- **Searching on the Series tab** finds a series by its own title, author, fandom or tags, and
  the count says how many series it found.

## Beta 202611090412 · 9 November 2026

- **Import from Files works again.** In the last beta, choosing Import from Files from the +
  menu, or from an empty library, did nothing. It opens Files again, for every kind of file.

## Beta 202611090248 · 9 November 2026

- **Series.** Fics that come in instalments can be grouped into a series, shown in your library
  as one entry: a stack of covers marked "Series", with its title, author, fandom and how many
  you've read ("Read 5 of 12"). Tap it to see its fics in order. You still read one fic at a
  time; a series just keeps them together.
- **Make a series your way.** Tap Select, pick the fics, then Series › Create Series. Drag them
  into the right order, remove any you picked by mistake, and choose which cover goes on top.
  Fics can be added to a series or taken out later, and the order changed at any time.
- **Auto Series.** FiloFic can look through your library for fics that belong together, using
  the "Part 3 of …" line archives put in their files, and says how many it found. Nothing is
  grouped until you accept it. New parts of a series you already have join it when you import
  them.
- **Series in search.** Search finds a series by its title, author, fandom or tags. Fics in a
  series say which part they are.
- **Series in your backups.** Exporting your library, and your device backups, keep your
  series as you arranged them.

## Beta 202611082113 · 8 November 2026

- **FiloFic in five more languages.** Spanish (Latin America), Brazilian Portuguese,
  Indonesian, Japanese and Korean, chosen in your iPhone's settings. Moods and tags show in your
  language, and you can search with those names too. Your own fics, tags and fandoms stay as
  you wrote them.
- **Backups you control.** Set a recovery passphrase in Settings › Backup and your library is
  included in your device's backups, still encrypted. On a new phone, or after a Face ID
  change, Recover Your Library brings it back with your passphrase. You can also keep the
  passphrase in iCloud Keychain. Nobody can open a backup without it, Apple included.
- **Export and import your library** as one encrypted file, to keep a copy or move to a new
  device.
- **Apple Intelligence is off until you turn it on.** No AI reads your fics or searches unless
  you choose to. Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence to have FiloFic suggest fandoms,
  ships, moods and summaries. You can remove everything it suggested at any time.
- **Authors' Rights and AI**, in Settings › About: we never train AI on fics, never collect
  them and never let them be collected.
- **Both policies in every language**, with dates in your language's style.

To start fresh with this beta, delete FiloFic and install it again from TestFlight, then
import your EPUBs. Libraries from the first betas can take a very long time to open.

## Beta 202611082030 · 8 November 2026

- **A steady scroll bar.** While you scroll, a slim bar at the edge shows how far through the
  whole fic you are. It stays the same size and never jumps as chapters load. EPUBs had no
  scroll bar at all before, and other fics had one that grew, shrank and jumped.
- **Pictures full size.** Hold your finger on a picture in an EPUB to see it on its own, then
  pinch or double-tap to zoom in.
- **The lamp comes on.** When FiloFic opens, the lamp in the logo starts switched off and
  gently lights up.
- **Release notes in the app.** Settings › About › Release Notes opens this page.
- **For beta testers: Share Debug Info.** If your library won't open, or opens empty, tap
  Share Debug Info on the lock screen (or in Settings › About) and send what it makes to the
  developer. It includes your encrypted library and the key that opens it, so send it only to
  the developer. This button isn't in the App Store version.
- **Credits.** Settings › About › Acknowledgements now lists every piece of open-source
  software inside the EPUB reader.

## Beta 202611081338 · 8 November 2026

**EPUBs now look the way they were made.** They're shown by Readium, the open-source engine
many reading apps use:

- each book keeps its own styling, pictures, fonts, poems and tables;
- footnotes open in a small sheet over the page;
- links inside the book take you to the right place;
- scrolling runs straight on from one chapter into the next, with a gap between them, as in
  Apple Books (you can switch to turning pages in each fic's reading settings);
- the original EPUB is kept, compressed, inside your encrypted library, and a book still can't
  fetch anything from the internet.

To get this for fics you already have, go to Settings › Clear Library, then import your EPUBs
again with **+ › Import from Files › EPUBs Only**. Fics imported before this beta don't have
their original file, so they open in FiloFic's own reader, and sharing one gives a copy
rebuilt from its text, without its pictures.

**Also fixed:**

- On iPad, Clear Library closes Settings first, so you land on your fresh library.
- On iPad, a reading milestone opens Settings at your reading stats.

## Beta 202611080859 · 8 November 2026

- **Reading:** scrolling runs on from one chapter into the next. Text is justified and
  hyphenated, with spacing like Apple Books and bold chapter headings. AO3's notes are set apart
  from the story, and the title page lists its tags with each label above its value. You can
  choose justified or ragged text in the reading settings.
- **Links:** web links in a fic, like AO3's "leave feedback", open in your own browser.
  FiloFic itself still never goes online.
- **Importing:** an import keeps going when you leave the app, and picks up where it stopped
  if it's interrupted. You can import just EPUBs, PDFs or text files, and see your library
  fill as the fics arrive. Anything already in your library, or in a format FiloFic can't
  read, is skipped and listed.
- **Fandoms:** a fic listed in several fandoms appears under each of them. Fandoms and ships
  can be merged and renamed (Fandoms › Merge). The Browse button is now called Fandoms.
- **Search:** results are grouped behind pills, with titles and authors first. Quoted phrases
  match exactly, and FiloFic Search needs everything you ask for, so "spicy horror" means
  both.
- **Settings:** Clear Library, at the bottom, deletes everything and asks twice first.
- **Fixed:** typing in search could freeze the app; swiping to delete a tag didn't work; the
  same fic could be imported twice.

## First betas · 7 November 2026

The first versions of FiloFic, in builds 202611071949 to 202611080549:

- an encrypted library that opens only with Face ID or Touch ID, and never goes online;
- importing EPUB, PDF, HTML, Markdown and text files, or whole folders, with the fandom, ship,
  rating, tags, summary and dates read from AO3's headers;
- fandom, ship and mood suggested by Apple Intelligence on the device, where it's available;
- browsing by fandom, then ship, then mood, and a shelf of summaries to skim;
- FiloFic Search: describe what you'd like to read in your own words;
- searching inside every fic, with the passage highlighted;
- your own rating scales, each with its own emoji;
- a reader with paper, light and dark themes, your choice of font and size, and words read
  counted with milestones;
- sharing a fic as an EPUB, or sending it to your Kindle.
